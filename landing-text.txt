=== 9. FAQ ===
faq.eyebrow: FAQ
faq.headline: Common questions
faq.q1: Do I need to install anything?
faq.a1: No. SO+ runs in your browser on a phone or computer. You need an internet connection, because the Excel and PDF files are made on the SO+ server.
faq.q2: How do I get an account?
faq.a2: Tap *Sign up* and enter your email, a password, name, designation, mobile, district and taluka. You can start using SO+ straight away; no approval is needed. Your 15-day free trial counts from the day you sign up.
faq.q3: Which SOR does SO+ use?
faq.a3: The R&B and GWSSB SOR of your district, including 1% labour cess. Upload your SOR in My Formats; the SO+ team sets it up and all users of the district use it. Until then the Kheda SOR is used. Each item shows its SOR reference.
faq.q4: Can I change an item, rate or GST?
faq.a4: Yes. In Dashboard › My Formats open the work, change items, rates, For Record or additions, and press Save My Format. Every new estimate uses it. You can also change any value in the downloaded Excel.
faq.q5: Is the estimate ready to submit as-is?
faq.a5: Treat it as a strong first draft. The engineer who signs the estimate must check it against the current SOR and office requirements. Acceptance is decided by your office.
faq.q6: Is SO+ an official government app?
faq.a6: No. SO+ is an independent tool made to help Taluka Panchayat engineers. It is not made or endorsed by the Government of Gujarat.
faq.q7: What does the free trial include?
faq.a7: For 15 days from sign-up you can make up to 25 estimates, 25 bills and 25 kachu MB (કાચું બિલ) for each work type, plus 25 forwarding letters. After the trial, your account becomes read-only and shows a page to contact support.
faq.q8: Can I add a work that is not in the list?
faq.a8: Yes. In My Formats type its name in the last row (Other), add its items from the SOR or a rate analysis, and save. It then works like the other works.
